# TV Radar

**Free UK TV guide: what's on Freeview tonight, channel by channel.**

TV Radar is an independent UK television guide at [tvradar.uk](https://tvradar.uk).
It is built from the public Freeview XMLTV feed, which covers about seven days
ahead. Sky listings are added only for pay-TV films and live football. All 32
channel guides rebuild every night and switch to the new day at midnight.

---

### What's on it

- **[What's on TV tonight](https://tvradar.uk/whats-on-tv-tonight)**: a daily guide to the night's best picks, with a page for each day of the week
- **Channel guides**: 32 Freeview channels, each with today's full schedule and highlights, from [BBC One](https://tvradar.uk/bbc-one-tv-guide) to Film4
- **[Films on TV tonight](https://tvradar.uk/films-on-tv-this-week)**: every film on free TV, with a timeline of tonight's, plus a [Sky Cinema guide](https://tvradar.uk/sky-cinema-tv-guide)
- **[Sport on TV today](https://tvradar.uk/sport-on-tv-today)**: [football](https://tvradar.uk/football-on-tv-today), the [Premier League](https://tvradar.uk/premier-league-on-tv), rugby, cricket and racing, mapped to channels
- **[What was on TV last night](https://tvradar.uk/what-was-on-tv-last-night)**: in case you missed it
- **[Shows](https://tvradar.uk/tv-shows)**: when the soaps and big series are on, and how to watch them

### How it's built

WordPress serves the site. The content lives as markdown and syncs to WordPress
from a TypeScript CLI. A nightly pipeline rebuilds each channel's schedule from
the feed, checks it, and publishes the next day's pages ahead of time.

### What we don't publish

We don't republish broadcaster programme synopses, and we don't paraphrase them
either. That text belongs to the broadcasters. Listings carry times, titles,
episode numbers, durations and channel numbers, which are facts. Every
description on TV Radar is written in our own words, and each night's copy is
checked against the feed for copied wording before it goes live.

The same goes for programme artwork: stills and posters belong to the people who
made them, so we don't use them. Channel logos appear only to identify the
channel.

Full detail: **[Editorial standards and corrections](https://tvradar.uk/editorial-standards)**.

### Independent

Not affiliated with the BBC, ITV, Channel 4, Channel 5, Sky or Freeview. No
broadcaster pays for placement. Pages that carry affiliate links say so.

and date. We fix mistakes in our own writing within a working day. If the error
came from a broadcaster's feed, we'll trace it and tell you what we find.
